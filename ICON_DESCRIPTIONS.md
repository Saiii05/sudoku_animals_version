# Animal Icon Set: Descriptions

This document provides a detailed description of the 9 animal icons for the Animal Sudoku game. The icons should be in a cute, clean, and consistent flat-design style, with soft pastel colors and rounded edges. They should be suitable for a mobile puzzle game UI, with a uniform size and transparent background.

### 1. Lion
- **Concept:** A friendly, approachable lion cub face.
- **Expression:** Gentle smile, with closed, happy eyes.
- **Mane:** Composed of soft, rounded, cloud-like shapes, not sharp or spiky.
- **Color Palette:** Soft golden yellow for the face, with a slightly darker pastel orange for the mane.
- **Details:** A small, pink, heart-shaped nose.

### 2. Elephant
- **Concept:** A cute, playful baby elephant.
- **Features:** Large, floppy ears and a trunk curled up in a friendly wave.
- **Expression:** Wide, curious eyes and a gentle smile.
- **Color Palette:** Light, pastel blue-gray for the body, with soft pink on the inside of the ears.
- **Details:** No tusks to maintain a soft, friendly look.

### 3. Monkey
- **Concept:** A cheeky, fun-loving monkey.
- **Features:** A heart-shaped face outline with large, expressive ears.
- **Expression:** A wide, mischievous grin, possibly with a hint of a pink tongue sticking out.
- **Color Palette:** A warm, light brown for the fur, with a lighter cream or beige for the face, ears, and belly.
- **Details:** A single, cute swirl of hair on top of the head.

### 4. Panda
- **Concept:** A sleepy, content panda cub.
- **Features:** The iconic black eye patches should be soft and rounded.
- **Expression:** A calm, gentle smile with closed or half-closed eyes.
- **Color Palette:** Off-white or very light cream for the face, with soft, charcoal-gray patches (not harsh black).
- **Details:** A small, simple, U-shaped mouth.

### 5. Tiger
- **Concept:** A playful, non-threatening tiger cub.
- **Features:** Soft, rounded stripes that are more like patterns than sharp stripes.
- **Expression:** A happy, open-mouthed smile.
- **Color Palette:** A soft, pastel orange for the fur, with muted, dark-brown stripes.
- **Details:** Cute, rounded ears and a small, pink nose.

### 6. Rabbit
- **Concept:** A fluffy, gentle bunny.
- **Features:** Long, floppy ears, one of which is slightly bent.
- **Expression:** A sweet, calm face with a small, V-shaped mouth and rosy cheeks.
- **Color Palette:** A soft, creamy white or very light gray for the fur, with pastel pink for the nose and inner ears.
- **Details:** Two small, simple buck teeth peeking out from under the mouth.

### 7. Owl
- **Concept:** A wise, yet cute, little owl.
- **Features:** Big, round, captivating eyes that take up a large portion of the face.
- **Expression:** A neutral, calm expression.
- **Color Palette:** A mix of soft, muted browns and grays, with a lighter, cream-colored belly.
- **Details:** Small, feathery tufts on the head that look like eyebrows.

### 8. Frog
- **Concept:** A cheerful, friendly frog.
- **Features:** Large, expressive eyes on top of the head.
- **Expression:** A wide, happy smile that stretches across the face.
- **Color Palette:** A bright, light, pastel green for the skin, with small, rosy pink circles on the cheeks.
- **Details:** The pupils of the eyes can be slightly different sizes to add character.

### 9. Cat
- **Concept:** A sweet, domestic kitten.
- **Features:** Pointed, triangular ears and long, simple whiskers.
- **Expression:** A contented, cat-like smile (like a ":3" shape).
- **Color Palette:** A soft, pastel gray or light, creamy orange (like a tabby cat).
- **Details:** A small, pink, heart-shaped nose.
